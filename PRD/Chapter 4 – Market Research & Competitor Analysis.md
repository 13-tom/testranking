# Chapter 4 – Market Research & Competitor Analysis

**Project:** Board Ranking

**Document Type:** Product Requirement Document (PRD)

**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Introduction

Before building any successful product, it is essential to understand the market.

This chapter analyzes the current education technology landscape, identifies the problems existing platforms have not solved, and explains how Board Ranking positions itself differently.

The objective is not to compete with every EdTech company in India.

The objective is to solve one problem exceptionally well.

---

# 2. Market Opportunity

India has one of the world's largest school education systems.

Every year, millions of students prepare for board examinations.

Our initial target audience includes:

* CBSE Class 9
* CBSE Class 10
* CBSE Class 11
* CBSE Class 12

This represents millions of students who regularly practice questions, prepare for examinations, and seek ways to measure their progress.

The demand for structured digital practice continues to increase every year.

---

# 3. The Problem in Today's Market

Most existing platforms focus on one of the following:

* Video lectures
* Coaching classes
* Doubt solving
* Competitive examinations (JEE/NEET/UPSC)
* Generic mock tests

Very few platforms focus on helping school students answer one important question:

> **"Am I improving, and how do I compare with students like me?"**

Students often know their marks but do not understand:

* Their weak chapters.
* Their learning progress.
* Their consistency.
* Their improvement over time.
* Their academic standing.

Board Ranking aims to solve these problems.

---

# 4. Existing Competitors

# 6. What Makes Board Ranking Different?

Board Ranking is designed around practice rather than content consumption.

Instead of asking students to watch more lectures, the platform encourages them to solve more questions.

Core differentiators include:

* Chapter-wise MCQ practice
* Subject-wise tests
* Detailed performance analytics
* Study Points system
* Progress tracking
* Transparent rankings
* Simple user experience
* Admin-curated question bank

---

# 7. Our Competitive Advantages

### Student-First Design

Every feature is designed around improving student learning.

---

### Simple User Experience

Students should be able to start practicing within minutes.

No unnecessary complexity.

---

### Practice-Oriented

Students learn by solving questions rather than passively consuming content.

---

### High-Quality Question Bank

---

# 14. Risks

Potential challenges include:

* Existing large competitors.
* Student acquisition.
* Maintaining question quality.
* Building trust in rankings.
* Retaining students over time.

Each challenge will be addressed through product quality rather than feature quantity.

---

# 15. Opportunity

The opportunity is significant because students need a platform that answers three simple questions after every practice session:

1. How did I perform?
2. Where do I need improvement?
3. What should I study next?

Board Ranking is designed to answer these questions better than any existing solution.

---

# 16. Success Strategy

Instead of becoming another large educational platform immediately, Board Ranking will focus on becoming exceptional at one thing:

> Helping students practice consistently and improve through measurable progress.

The company will expand only after this foundation has been successfully validated.

---

# CTO Statement

The education market already contains excellent platforms for teaching.

Board Ranking is not entering the market to teach students.

It is entering the market to help students measure, understand, and continuously improve their preparation.

By focusing on one problem and solving it exceptionally well, Board Ranking can build a strong and sustainable position in the education ecosystem.
