# Chapter 1 – Executive Summary

**Project Name:** Board Ranking

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Product Requirement Document (PRD)

**Current Product Stage:** MVP Planning

**Target Launch:** Release 1.0

---

# 1. Introduction

Board Ranking is a student-first online assessment platform designed for students of **Class 9, 10, 11, and 12**.

The platform enables students to practice chapter-wise and subject-wise MCQ tests, analyze their performance, improve weak topics, earn Study Points, and track their academic progress through rankings and detailed analytics.

Unlike traditional test websites that only provide scores, Board Ranking focuses on creating a complete learning ecosystem where every test helps students improve.

The first release is intentionally simple and focused. The objective is to build an excellent testing experience before expanding into a larger educational platform.

---

# 2. Vision

To become India's most trusted academic practice and ranking platform for school students by making learning measurable, engaging, and data-driven.

Students should no longer wonder whether they are prepared for their examinations.

They should know exactly where they stand, what they need to improve, and what to study next.

---

# 3. Mission

Board Ranking exists to solve one fundamental problem:

> Students practice regularly but rarely receive meaningful feedback about their actual academic preparation.

Our mission is to provide every student with:

* High-quality practice tests
* Instant evaluation
* Meaningful performance analysis
* Personalized improvement recommendations
* Transparent ranking
* Consistent motivation through Study Points and achievements

---

# 4. Problem Statement

Today, most students prepare using books, coaching material, YouTube videos, or mock tests.

However, these methods have several limitations:

* Students don't know whether they are improving.
* Students don't know how they compare with others.
* Weak chapters remain unidentified.
* Practice is often inconsistent.
* Motivation decreases over time.
* Progress is difficult to measure.

Current platforms focus heavily on content but often fail to create a habit of regular practice.

---

# 5. Our Solution

Board Ranking combines practice, analysis, and motivation into one platform.

Every completed test contributes toward:

* Better understanding
* Improved accuracy
* Higher Study Points
* Better rankings
* Stronger study habits

Instead of simply answering questions, students continuously build an academic profile that reflects their overall progress.

---

# 6. Product Philosophy

Every feature in Board Ranking must answer one simple question:

> Does this help students learn better?

If the answer is no, the feature does not belong in the MVP.

The product follows five core principles:

### Student First

Every decision prioritizes student learning over unnecessary features.

### Simplicity

The platform should feel easy enough for a first-time student to understand without any guidance.

### Speed

Students should be able to begin practicing within minutes.

### Quality

A smaller collection of high-quality questions is better than a massive collection of poorly reviewed questions.

### Trust

Scores, rankings, and analytics must always be accurate and reliable.

---

# 7. Target Audience

Release 1 focuses on:

* CBSE Class 9 Students
* CBSE Class 10 Students
* CBSE Class 11 Students
* CBSE Class 12 Students

Future releases may support additional education boards.

---

# 8. MVP Objective

The goal of Release 1 is **not** to build a complete EdTech platform.

The goal is to validate one hypothesis:

> If students receive high-quality MCQ practice, instant feedback, Study Points, and meaningful rankings, they will return consistently to improve their performance.

Everything included in the MVP supports this objective.

---

# 9. Core Features of Release 1

The MVP includes only the features necessary to deliver the core learning experience.

### Student Module

* Mobile Number + OTP Login
* Student Registration
* Profile Completion
* Student Dashboard
* Chapter-wise Tests
* Subject-wise Tests
* Test History
* Performance Analysis
* Rankings
* Study Points
* Study Levels
* Achievements
* Subscription Management
* Profile Settings

### Admin Module

* Secure Admin Login
* Student Management
* Question Bank Management
* Bulk Question Upload
* Duplicate Question Detection
* Test Creation
* Test Management
* Reports
* Analytics
* Subscription Management
* Question Review
* System Settings

---

# 10. Features Excluded from Release 1

The following features are intentionally postponed:

* Teacher Dashboard
* Parent Dashboard
* School Management
* Friend System
* Friend Battles
* School Battles
* Live Tests
* AI Question Generator
* AI Answer Evaluation
* Community Features
* Offline Kit Marketplace
* Video Learning
* Discussion Forums

These features will be introduced only after the core platform is stable.

---

# 11. Success Criteria

Release 1 will be considered successful if students can:

* Register successfully
* Log in securely
* Complete their profile
* Attempt tests without issues
* Receive instant results
* View accurate analytics
* Track Study Points
* Check rankings
* Return regularly for additional practice

Success is measured by student engagement rather than the number of features.

---

# 12. Product Identity

Board Ranking is **not** a coaching institute.

Board Ranking is **not** a video learning platform.

Board Ranking is **not** a social network.

Board Ranking is a **practice-first academic improvement platform**.

Its purpose is to help students improve through consistent testing, measurable progress, and meaningful feedback.

---

# 13. Guiding Principle

Every feature, every screen, every API, and every database table should support one mission:

> Help students practice consistently, understand their weaknesses, improve their performance, and build confidence before their examinations.

This principle should guide every future product decision.

---

# CTO Notes

The first release deliberately avoids unnecessary complexity.

Instead of building every possible feature, Release 1 focuses on doing a few important things exceptionally well.

A strong foundation is more valuable than a large feature list.

Every future version of Board Ranking will build upon this foundation without compromising the product's core philosophy of simplicity, quality, and student-first learning.
