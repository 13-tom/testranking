# Board Ranking Privacy Policy

At Board Ranking, protecting the privacy and security of our students is one of our highest priorities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to our users.

By registering for or using Board Ranking, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

# 1. Our Privacy Commitment

Board Ranking is built with a **Privacy by Design** approach.

We collect only the information necessary to provide educational services.

We do **not** sell student data to advertisers or third parties.

Student trust is one of our most valuable assets.

---

# 2. Information We Collect

## A. Registration Information

When a student creates an account, we collect:

* Full Name
* Mobile Number
* Class (9, 10, 11, or 12)
* School Name
* State
* District

Optional information:

* Profile Photo
* Gender

---

## B. Academic Information

While using Board Ranking, we collect:

* Tests Attempted
* Test Scores
* Correct Answers
* Incorrect Answers
* Accuracy
* Time Taken
* Study Points
* Study Level
* Achievements
* Rankings
* Learning Progress

This information is used only to improve the student's learning experience.

---

## C. Technical Information

To maintain security and platform performance, we may collect:

* Device Type
* Browser Information
* Operating System
* IP Address
* Login Time
* Logout Time
* Session Information
* Error Logs

This information helps us diagnose technical problems and protect the platform.

---

## D. Payment Information

If a student purchases a subscription:

Board Ranking does **not** store:

* Debit Card Numbers
* Credit Card Numbers
* CVV
* UPI PIN

Payments are securely processed through trusted third-party payment providers.

Only limited transaction details such as payment status, transaction reference, subscription plan, and purchase date may be stored.

---

# 3. Information We Do NOT Collect

Board Ranking does not intentionally collect:

* Aadhaar Number
* PAN Number
* Bank Account Details
* Passwords (MVP uses OTP login)
* Biometric Data
* Face Recognition Data
* Voice Samples
* GPS Location
* Contacts
* SMS Messages

---

# 4. Why We Collect Information

Information is collected to:

* Create student accounts.
* Verify identity using OTP.
* Generate rankings.
* Calculate Study Points.
* Track learning progress.
* Recommend practice tests.
* Improve platform performance.
* Provide customer support.
* Prevent fraud and abuse.

We collect only information that supports the educational objectives of the platform.

---

# 5. How We Use Information

Student information may be used to:

* Authenticate login.
* Display dashboard information.
* Generate analytics.
* Maintain rankings.
* Improve question recommendations.
* Detect suspicious activity.
* Improve product quality.
* Respond to support requests.

Board Ranking never uses personal information for unrelated advertising purposes.

---

# 6. How We Protect Information

Board Ranking follows industry-standard security practices.

Examples include:

* HTTPS Encryption
* Secure JWT Authentication
* OTP Verification
* Backend Validation
* Database Access Controls
* Role-Based Authorization
* Rate Limiting
* Secure Server Infrastructure
* Audit Logging

Sensitive operations are always performed on the backend.

---

# 7. Data Storage

Student information is stored securely on protected cloud infrastructure.

Only authorized systems and authorized administrators may access operational data required to provide the service.

Database backups are performed regularly to reduce the risk of accidental data loss.

---

# 8. Data Sharing

Board Ranking does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only when necessary with trusted service providers, such as:

* OTP/SMS providers
* Payment gateways
* Cloud hosting providers
* Analytics and monitoring services

These providers receive only the information necessary to perform their services.

---

# 9. Student Rankings

Certain academic information may be displayed inside the platform.

Examples include:

* Student Name
* Study Level
* Study Points
* Overall Rank
* Class Rank

No sensitive personal information is displayed publicly.

Students are never shown another student's mobile number or other private personal information.

---

# 10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Board Ranking may use cookies or similar technologies to:

* Maintain login sessions.
* Improve performance.
* Remember preferences.
* Analyze platform usage.

Users may manage cookie settings through their browser, although disabling essential cookies may affect platform functionality.

---

# 11. Data Retention

Board Ranking retains information only as long as necessary to:

* Maintain student accounts.
* Preserve academic history.
* Meet legal obligations.
* Improve educational services.

Inactive accounts may eventually be archived according to internal data retention policies.

---

# 12. User Rights

Students may:

* View their profile.
* Update profile information.
* View academic history.
* Contact support regarding account information.

Requests related to account deletion or applicable privacy rights will be handled in accordance with Indian law and operational requirements.

---

# 13. Children's Privacy

Board Ranking is intended for school students.

Where applicable, students should use the platform with the knowledge or permission of their parent or legal guardian.

We take additional care to protect the information of younger users.

---

# 14. Third-Party Services

Board Ranking may integrate with trusted third-party services, including:

* SMS Gateway
* Payment Gateway
* Cloud Hosting Provider
* Monitoring and Logging Tools

Each provider is responsible for protecting information handled within its own services.

---

# 15. Security Incidents

If a significant security incident affecting personal information occurs, Board Ranking will:

* Investigate promptly.
* Contain the issue.
* Restore affected systems.
* Notify affected users where required by applicable law.
* Improve security controls to prevent recurrence.

---

# 16. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically as the platform evolves or legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on the official Board Ranking website.

Continued use of the platform after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

---

# 17. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the official support channels published on the Board Ranking website.

---

# 18. Privacy Principles

Board Ranking follows these core privacy principles:

* Collect only necessary information.
* Protect student data.
* Never sell personal information.
* Be transparent about data usage.
* Secure information using modern security practices.
* Respect student privacy.
* Continuously improve security.

---

# 19. Future Privacy Enhancements

Future versions of Board Ranking may include:

* Download My Data
* Account Deletion Request
* Privacy Dashboard
* Device Management
* Login History
* Session Management
* Consent Management

These features will be introduced as the platform grows.

---

# CTO Privacy Statement

Privacy is not simply a legal requirement—it is a promise to every student who trusts Board Ranking.

Every piece of information collected must have a clear educational purpose.

Every system must be designed with privacy and security as core principles, not as features added later.

Our goal is to build a platform where students, parents, and schools can trust that academic progress and personal information are handled responsibly, securely, and transparently.
