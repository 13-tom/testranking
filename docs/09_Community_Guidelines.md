# Board Ranking Community Guidelines

## 1. Introduction

Board Ranking is an educational platform built to help students learn, practice, and improve academically.

Our community is based on three core values:

* Respect
* Honesty
* Continuous Learning

Every student, administrator, and future community member is expected to follow these guidelines.

---

# 2. Our Mission

We believe education should be:

* Fair
* Honest
* Safe
* Supportive

Board Ranking is designed to motivate students through healthy competition, not unhealthy rivalry.

---

# 3. Respect Every Student

Every student deserves respect regardless of:

* Marks
* Rank
* School
* State
* Learning speed
* Academic performance

Bullying, harassment, or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated.

---

# 4. Honest Learning

Students should always attempt tests honestly.

Do not:

* Use unfair methods during tests.
* Attempt to manipulate scores.
* Create multiple accounts.
* Share answers to gain unfair advantages.
* Use bots or automation.

The purpose of Board Ranking is improvement, not shortcuts.

---

# 5. Fair Competition

Rankings should reflect genuine academic performance.

Students must never attempt to:

* Manipulate rankings.
* Exploit bugs.
* Cheat during tests.
* Abuse platform features.

Any unfair advantage harms the learning experience for everyone.

---

# 6. Reporting Incorrect Questions

Students are encouraged to report:

* Wrong answers.
* Typographical mistakes.
* Incorrect explanations.
* Out-of-syllabus questions.
* Ambiguous questions.

Reports help improve the quality of the Question Bank.

False or abusive reports may be ignored or investigated.

---

# 7. Account Responsibility

Students are responsible for:

* Keeping their mobile number secure.
* Never sharing OTPs.
* Protecting their account.
* Logging out from shared devices.

Board Ranking is not responsible for issues caused by sharing account access.

---

# 8. Platform Integrity

Students must not attempt to:

* Hack the platform.
* Reverse engineer the software.
* Access restricted areas.
* Modify website requests.
* Use automated scripts.
* Interfere with platform performance.

Such actions may result in immediate suspension.

---

# 9. Respect Intellectual Property

The Question Bank is one of Board Ranking's most valuable assets.

Students may not:

* Copy questions.
* Sell questions.
* Publish questions elsewhere.
* Redistribute platform content without permission.

Educational use within the platform is permitted.

---

# 10. Appropriate Behaviour

Students should:

* Learn respectfully.
* Use polite language when contacting support.
* Report issues honestly.
* Encourage healthy competition.

Board Ranking promotes positive academic growth.

---

# 11. Consequences of Violations

Depending on the severity of the violation, Board Ranking may:

* Issue a warning.
* Remove Study Points.
* Reset rankings.
* Suspend the account.
* Permanently ban the account.
* Take legal action where appropriate.

Actions will be taken fairly and consistently.

---

# 12. Appeals

If a student believes an action was taken in error, they may contact the official support team.

Every appeal will be reviewed individually.

Submitting an appeal does not guarantee that a decision will be reversed.

---

# 13. Future Community Features

As Board Ranking grows, new community features may include:

* Discussion forums.
* Friend challenges.
* Study groups.
* School competitions.
* Leaderboards.
* Community events.

These features will follow the same principles of respect, honesty, and fairness.

---

# 14. Community Principles

Every member of the Board Ranking community should:

* Learn honestly.
* Compete fairly.
* Respect others.
* Report problems responsibly.
* Help improve the platform.

Together, we create a better learning environment for everyone.

---

# CTO Community Statement

Board Ranking is more than a testing platform.

It is a learning community built on trust.

Every ranking, every Study Point, and every achievement should represent genuine effort.

We believe students grow the most when they compete fairly, respect one another, and focus on continuous improvement rather than shortcuts.

Our goal is to build a community where success is earned through learning, discipline, and integrity.
