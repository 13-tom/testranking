# Board Ranking Fair Play Policy

## 1. Introduction

Board Ranking is built on one fundamental principle:

> **Every rank should be earned through genuine learning and honest effort.**

The purpose of this Fair Play Policy is to protect the integrity of our platform and ensure that every student's achievements, Study Points, and rankings accurately reflect their academic performance.

All users are expected to follow this policy while using Board Ranking.

---

# 2. Our Fair Play Principles

Board Ranking is committed to:

* Honest learning
* Fair competition
* Equal opportunities
* Accurate rankings
* Secure assessments
* Transparent evaluation

Every technical and business decision supports these principles.

---

# 3. Why Fair Play Matters

Students trust Board Ranking because:

* Rankings are meaningful.
* Results are accurate.
* Every student competes under the same rules.

Without fair play, rankings lose credibility and the platform loses its educational value.

---

# 4. One Student, One Account

Each student may have only **one active account**.

Students must not:

* Create multiple accounts.
* Register using different mobile numbers to gain advantages.
* Share accounts with others.

If duplicate accounts are detected, Board Ranking may suspend or permanently remove them.

---

# 5. Honest Test Attempts

Students should attempt every test independently.

Students must not:

* Copy answers from others during ranked assessments.
* Use unauthorized tools to manipulate scores.
* Share answers to obtain unfair advantages.
* Use automated software during tests.

Practice Mode is intended for learning, while Ranked Mode is intended for fair competition.

---

# 6. Rankings

Rankings are generated entirely by the backend.

Students cannot influence rankings through:

* Browser modifications.
* API manipulation.
* Device changes.
* Editing frontend code.
* Automated requests.

Any attempt to manipulate rankings will be treated as a serious violation.

---

# 7. Study Points

Study Points are awarded only through approved platform activities.

Examples include:

* Completing profile sections.
* Completing tests.
* Unlocking achievements.
* Participating in approved learning activities.

Study Points cannot be purchased, transferred, or manually increased by users.

---

# 8. Platform Security

Students must not attempt to:

* Reverse engineer the platform.
* Discover hidden APIs.
* Modify requests.
* Inject malicious code.
* Access administrative features.
* Exploit software vulnerabilities.

Security violations may result in immediate account suspension.

---

# 9. Question Protection

The Question Bank is protected intellectual property.

Students may not:

* Copy questions.
* Record large portions of the Question Bank.
* Redistribute platform content.
* Sell educational material obtained from Board Ranking.

Unauthorized reproduction of platform content is prohibited.

---

# 10. Bug Reporting

If a student discovers a software bug, they should report it responsibly.

Students must not:

* Exploit bugs repeatedly.
* Share exploit methods publicly.
* Use bugs to gain unfair rankings.

Responsible reporting helps improve the platform for everyone.

---

# 11. Suspicious Activity

Board Ranking continuously monitors for unusual behaviour.

Examples include:

* Multiple accounts.
* Abnormally fast submissions.
* Automated activity.
* Repeated suspicious login attempts.
* Unusual ranking changes.

Detection does not automatically result in penalties.

Each case is reviewed before action is taken.

---

# 12. Administrative Actions

Depending on the severity of a violation, Board Ranking may:

* Issue a warning.
* Remove Study Points.
* Reset rankings.
* Invalidate test attempts.
* Suspend the account temporarily.
* Permanently terminate the account.

Repeated violations may result in permanent removal from the platform.

---

# 13. Appeals Process

Students who believe an action was taken incorrectly may contact the official support team.

Every appeal will be reviewed fairly.

Providing false information during an appeal may result in additional action.

---

# 14. Fair Competition Standards

Board Ranking believes that:

* Improvement is more important than winning.
* Learning is more important than shortcuts.
* Honest effort is more valuable than inflated scores.

Students are encouraged to compete with integrity.

---

# 15. Future Anti-Cheat Measures

As the platform grows, additional protection may include:

* Advanced behavioural analysis.
* AI-assisted anomaly detection.
* Device fingerprinting.
* Risk scoring.
* Improved session monitoring.

These measures will be introduced only when necessary and with appropriate privacy safeguards.

---

# 16. Our Commitment

Board Ranking commits to:

* Evaluating every student fairly.
* Calculating rankings accurately.
* Protecting student achievements.
* Investigating suspicious behaviour objectively.
* Continuously improving platform security.

Students can trust that honest effort will always be valued.

---

# 17. Fair Play Principles

Every student should remember:

* Learn honestly.
* Compete fairly.
* Respect other learners.
* Report problems responsibly.
* Earn achievements through genuine effort.

Fair competition benefits the entire learning community.

---

# CTO Fair Play Statement

Board Ranking is not built to reward shortcuts.

It is built to reward discipline, consistency, and continuous improvement.

Every Study Point, achievement, badge, and ranking should represent real academic effort.

We believe the true value of a student's progress comes not from defeating others, but from becoming better than they were yesterday.

Our responsibility is to ensure that every student competes in a secure, transparent, and trustworthy environment where success is earned—not manipulated.
